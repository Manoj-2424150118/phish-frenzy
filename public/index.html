📖 How to play

The whole game in 20 seconds:

The goal. Most points wins. A game is 10 questions, everyone answers the same one at the same time, and the podium at the end crowns the winner. 🏆

Scoring. A correct answer is worth 100 pts, plus up to 100 more for speed, plus a 25-pt bonus when you hit 3 correct in a row — that's the 🔥 beside a name. You get 25 seconds per question, and one tap locks your answer in for good.

The room. The 👑 host (whoever created the room) presses Start and Play again. Up to 12 players fit. Joining mid-game? You watch this one and play from the next. Your room code stays alive while your group is playing and is deleted only after the game ends and everyone has left — anyone with the code can join, so share it with your group only.

If you drop. Rejoin the same room with the same name and your seat and score are yours again.
